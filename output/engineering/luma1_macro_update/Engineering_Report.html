3. What the Macro File Supports

The actuator attachment is dated 8 November 2026, tested at 48 V with a 20 A peak current limit. Peak output torque = 2.982114349 × 20 = 59.6423 N·m [CALC], which is 9.63% below the former 66 N·m simulation ceiling. This is a calculated peak, not an experimentally established continuous torque rating. The export summary reports 10.682 rad/s no-load speed; the direct speed-test record reports 10.493 rad/s.

Handling of Defective Fitted Values [RULE 6]
The export recorded negative reflected inertia (−0.16098 kg·m²), negative Coulomb friction (−0.81061 N·m), and an invalid friction-fit R² of −0.5066. These were not silently corrected by flipping signs. Instead, the simulation treated positive values (0.16 kg·m² inertia, 0.81 N·m friction, 0.25 N·m·s/rad damping) as explicit sensitivity bounds, sweeping inertia from 0.05 to 0.25 kg·m² and friction up to 1.2 N·m.

4. Search and Optimization Method

A finite optimization evaluated 112 candidate trials (105 distinct configurations): a coarse grid/random search followed by local refinement. Each candidate ran two 9-second trials with the original policy. Variables included thigh/shin rod lengths, front/rear spring rates, damping, preload, and stroke.

Feasibility required: zero falls, no gantry activation, commanded 0.30 m/s producing 0.22–0.38 m/s forward velocity, lateral drift ≤ 0.06 m/s, at least 2.0 mm shock-stop clearance, torso tilt ≤ 20°, and ≤ 2% leg-motor torque saturation. Final validation executed 56 trials totaling 759 simulated seconds with 0 falls.

6. Operating Envelope and Stress Boundary

The robot prototype is specified for a 0.30 m/s level-ground cruising speed. Demo command limits have been restricted to: forward [−0.15, +0.40] m/s, lateral ±0.10 m/s, and turning ±0.30 rad/s.

In stress testing with 64 strong push disturbances (Δv = 0.3 to 0.9 m/s) and 60 seconds of continuous random roaming, 16/64 extreme push trials resulted in falls, and surviving high-impact cases overtraveled the simulated soft shock stops by up to 4.45 mm. These severe disturbances fall outside the recommended operating envelope and establish the physical boundaries for level-ground walking.
